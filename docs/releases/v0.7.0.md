## Feature

- Dictate on Windows with Right Alt or on Android with the microphone button, with model, language and text cleanup settings that sync across devices.
- Preview speech in real time with supported Gemini models, or recognize a complete recording in one request with file-based models.
- Track dictated Chinese characters and English words separately from typing speed.

## Feature

- Windows 按右 Alt、Android 点击麦克风即可语音输入，模型、语言和文字整理设置支持跨设备同步。
- 支持的 Gemini 模型可实时预览识别文字，文件识别模型则在录音结束后一次性识别。
- 单独统计语音输入的中文字数和英文单词数，不影响打字速度统计。
