## Feature

- Open Settings from the input method menu to switch between Full Pinyin and Xiaohe Shuangpin and manage updates.

## Improvement

- The language bar now shows the retype logo beside the Chinese or English mode indicator, and the candidate window uses a smaller horizontal layout.
- Tap Shift to switch languages and use `-` or `+` to change candidate pages.

## Fix

- More characters and phrases remain available in Xiaohe Shuangpin, with more accurate ordering for uncommon pronunciations.
